Physics Outreach Event

Physics in Juvenile

A science awareness and motivational session for young residents, bringing atoms, medicine and the quantum world into the room.

WhereJuvenile Correctional Facility
Duration1.5 – 2 hours
SpeakersNED University & University of Karachi
AfterwardsSweets for everyone
About the event

Curiosity, for everyone

Young residents get a friendly, non-technical look at physics through simple, relatable talks from university students who volunteer their time. The aim is to spark curiosity, encourage constructive thinking, and offer a positive educational break from routine facility life.

TALK 01

Particle Physics

An easy introduction to atoms, particles, and the building blocks of matter.

TALK 02

Medical Physics

How physics is used in hospitals and medicine: X-rays, imaging, radiation therapy.

TALK 03

Quantum Physics

A simplified, fascinating look into the quantum world.

Inspiration

Where the idea came from

This activity was inspired by Dr. Gediminas Šarpis, who ran Particle Physics in Prisons at Kaunas Prison in Lithuania, together with the NGO Inscribed Hands, which brings volunteers into prisons to provide activities and training.

Read more: Inscribed Hands, Particle Physics in Prisons →

Good to know

Goals & logistics

Goals

  • Introduce fundamental and modern physics in a fun, non-technical way.
  • Inspire curiosity, critical thinking and a positive outlook toward learning.
  • Offer a constructive activity that supports the facility's educational efforts.
  • Create friendly interaction between university students and young residents.

What we need

  • Coordination with facility administration for scheduling and access.
  • A hall or common room with seating.
  • Projector/screen and microphone, if available.
  • Sweets for the end of the session.
  • Facility staff present throughout.
